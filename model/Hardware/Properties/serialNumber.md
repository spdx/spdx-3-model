# serialNumber

## Summary

A unique identifier assigned to a specific physical instance of a product.

## Description

Serial Number is a specific identifier assigned to an individual product unit. It uniquely distinguishes that unit from all other instances of the same product, even when they share the same part number. Serial numbers are paired with a `partNumber` to fully specify a unique item.
