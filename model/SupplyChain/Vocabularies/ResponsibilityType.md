# ResponsibilityType

## Summary

Specifies the type of responsibility held over an asset, property, or entity.

## Description

ResponsibilityType categorizes the kind of authority or obligation that a
party has over an asset, property, or legal entity in a supply chain
context.

## Metadata

- name: ResponsibilityType

## Entries

- custody: Custody refers to the responsibility, control, and safekeeping of an asset, person, or legal entity. It involves both physical possession and legal accountability over something.
- ownership: Ownership refers to the legal right to control, manage, and benefit from an asset, resource, or responsibility. It establishes authority, accountability, and entitlements over something, whether it's property, a business, intellectual property, or responsibilities.
