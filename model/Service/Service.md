# Service

## Summary

The Service profile describes software made available as a network-accessible service.

## Description

The Service profile defines concepts related to network-accessible services and their operational context. It enables service providers, consumers, auditors, and procurement organizations to identify a service, describe its provider and hosting locations, and represent dependencies on other services.

Service information can be combined with information from other SPDX namespaces to support service assurance use cases. These include evaluating software and service dependencies, vulnerability exposure, data governance, service provider identity, transmission security, availability, and regulatory compliance.

The Service profile applies to services accessed over a network rather than software artifacts distributed for local installation. It supports sharing relevant service metadata across organizational trust boundaries without requiring disclosure of internal implementation details.
