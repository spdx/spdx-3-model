# ControlType

## Summary

Controls may be of different types differentiated by ControlType.

## Description

A control may be of technical, physical, or organizational type. ControlType encodes this.

## Metadata

- name: ControlType

## Entries

- technical: technical controls are established by technical means, i.e. mechanics, hardware or software
- physical: physical controls are established by walls, fences, doors, locks or other tangible barriers or structures
- organizational: policies, rules, administrative procedures, and management frameworks that define security 
  expectations and govern human behavior.
